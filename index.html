    What is txtshell?

    A fast, local-first text editor. Everything stays on your device — no accounts, no cloud, no tracking. Just type and save.

    The core loop

    Write. Type anything in the editor. A block is one note — no title, no formatting, no folders.

    Save. Press ⌘/Ctrl + ⏎, or tap Save block below the editor.

    Find. ⌘/Ctrl + K (or tap Saved (N)) opens search. Or type a word followed by // in the editor and press ⏎.

    Edit. In search, hit Edit on a block — or select it and press Shift + ⏎ — change it, and save again.

    — the same four steps as short cards.

    Organise as you go

    Put #tags and @mentions anywhere in a block — search understands them, and several words together match only blocks containing all of them.

    /t, /y and /w show today’s, yesterday’s and last week’s blocks. ☆ on a block pins it; /pin shows only pinned blocks.

    /re reopens your most recent block.

    Keep it yours

    /encrypt locks your blocks with a passphrase that never leaves your device. /export downloads a backup (/export plain for plaintext) and /import restores one.

    Go further

    /q opens the full reference — every command and shortcut, grouped by task.

    txtshell

    A fast, local-first notes app.

    Your notes are encrypted with a key that never leaves your device.

    free · no account

    where notes live

    In your browser, encrypted. Not on a server first — so every keystroke, search, and save is instant.

    who holds the key

    Only your device. Made from your passphrase or your device's sign-in, never uploaded, never ours to hold.

    what a server sees

    Encrypted bytes with no key attached. Nothing to read, breach, or hand over — including for us.

    the experience

    Text at the speed of thought.

    instant

    Everything runs locally, so typing, saving, and searching never wait on a network. No spinners.

    keyboard-driven

    Slash commands do everything — /w for this week, /search, /font. No menus to hunt.

    just text

    Plain text that stays yours. Search across every note instantly; export it anytime.
