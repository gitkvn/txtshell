Who we are

Txtshell is run by Kavan Ravi, an individual based in Hyderabad, India. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean Kavan Ravi.

  • Contact for anything in this policy: hello@txtshell.com
  • Grievance officer (India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023): Kavan Ravi, hello@txtshell.com. We reply within 30 days.

The short version

  • Txtshell works without an account. Then your notes stay in your browser and we never receive them.
  • If you use cloud sync, your notes are end-to-end encrypted on your device before they are uploaded. We store only the encrypted copy and can’t read it.
  • We keep the minimum we need to run your account and your subscription, and we list all of it below.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, no third-party scripts, and no tracking in our emails.
  • Payments are handled by Dodo Payments. We never see your card or bank details.

Using Txtshell without an account

  • Your notes are stored in your browser (IndexedDB) on your device. We never receive them.
  • They are not encrypted by default. Anyone with access to your browser profile can read them. You can encrypt them on your device with a passphrase (/encrypt), or by using an account.
  • Preferences such as your theme are kept in your browser’s local storage.
  • No cookies. Txtshell sets none.
  • Deleting: delete notes in the app, or erase everything with /wipe. Clearing your browser’s site data also removes them.

Using cloud sync (an account)

Your notes

  • Encrypted on your device with a key we never receive, using passkeys and your 12-word recovery phrase. We store only the encrypted copy, and we can’t read your notes or their contents.
  • We can see technical details of the encrypted copy: its size, when it was uploaded, and which of your devices uploaded it.
  • If you lose every passkey and your 12 words, nobody can recover your notes, including us.

Abuse prevention

To stop abuse (for example, too many sign-in attempts), we count requests per IP address and per email address for a short time. These counters store a one-way keyed hash, not the address itself, and are deleted within about a day.

Like any website, Cloudflare (our host) processes your IP address and browser details to deliver each request and protect the service. We don’t receive or keep a log of them.

Unfinished sign-ups

If you start creating an account but don’t finish it (no passkey added), we delete your email address and anything linked to it after 7 days, unless you’ve already paid.

The waitlist

If you join the waitlist, we keep only your email address, to invite you. It is deleted when you confirm that address to create an account, or after 12 months, whichever comes first. Write to us to be removed sooner.

Emails we send

All are about your account or your subscription. We send no marketing.

  • Sign-in codes.
  • Security emails: a new device was added to your account; someone asked to erase your cloud notes; that request was stopped.
  • Renewal reminder: 30 days before your subscription renews.
  • Deletion warnings: 30 days and 7 days before a lapsed account’s cloud data is deleted.

Dodo Payments also emails you directly: receipts, renewal and payment notices, and reminders if a payment fails. Its emails are covered by its own privacy policy.

Payments

  • Dodo Payments is our merchant of record. It sells you the subscription, takes payment, collects the tax, and issues your invoice.
  • You give Dodo Payments your name, email, billing country or address and payment details on its own pages. It handles them under its own privacy policy, and keeps invoices and tax records as the law requires.
  • We send Dodo Payments your email address and our internal account identifier, so a payment can be matched to your account.
  • We receive from Dodo Payments the status of your subscription and payments.

How long we keep things after you stop

  • Your notes on your devices stay there until you delete them. Nothing we do removes them.
  • When a subscription ends (cancelled and the paid year over, lapsed after a failed payment, or refunded): sync pauses, and your encrypted copy and account are kept for at least 12 months so you can pick up where you left off. Then they are deleted. We email you 30 days and 7 days before.
  • Deleting your account (/account delete in the app) deletes your account and every encrypted copy at once, including earlier copies. Short-lived records, such as abuse-prevention counters and payment events, expire on their own as listed above. Dodo Payments keeps its own records of your purchases.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to withdraw consent where we rely on it, by writing to hello@txtshell.com. You can delete your account and its cloud data yourself, at any time, in the app (/account delete).

If you’re not satisfied with our reply, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India, or, if you’re in the EU or UK, to your local data protection authority.

We use your data to provide the service you asked for, to keep it secure and prevent abuse, and where the law requires it.

Children

Accounts and subscriptions are for people 18 or older. Txtshell without an account collects nothing about anyone.

Security

End-to-end encryption, passkey sign-in (no passwords), encryption in transit (HTTPS), and an email and in-app notice whenever a new device is added to your account. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

The browser extension

The Txtshell browser extension has its own privacy notice at txtshell.com/privacy-extension. It sends nothing to any server itself. What you capture with it becomes a note in Txtshell, and syncs only if you use cloud sync.

Changes to this policy

We’ll update the date above when this policy changes. For a significant change we’ll email account holders before it takes effect.
